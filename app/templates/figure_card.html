What this page does not know

  • Every step is a rule somebody wrote. The rules are all readable, and following the links above is how you check they say what this page says they say.

Carbon Paper turns an investigation into a workflow where the deterministic steps are predictable and the judgement calls are visible, so a published number can be followed back to the rows it came from. This page is that link.
