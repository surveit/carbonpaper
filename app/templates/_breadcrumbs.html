{# The header trail. Context: crumbs (list[app.web.breadcrumbs.Crumb]). Three kinds of rung, decided by the Crumb alone: one carrying `picker` is a SWITCHER — a button whose popover loads that partial the first time it is opened (static/breadcrumbs.js) — one carrying `href` is a link, and one carrying neither is the page you are on. A switcher rung names something the reader might have meant a different one of; a section rung never does. The FIRST rung is home, and it renders the wordmark in place of its label. A page whose address continues past its crumbs passes `crumb_tail`, the template holding those further rungs.
