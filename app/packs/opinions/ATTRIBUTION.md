# CourtListener opinions

The `courtlistener_opinions` connector reads opinion search results from the CourtListener REST
API (v4) and opinion PDFs from storage.courtlistener.com. Free Law Project runs both.

Terms: https://wiki.free.law/c/terms/courtlistener/courtlistenercom-terms-of-service-and-policies

Filings are generally in the public domain, but they may contain third-party copyrighted works.
Do not present the data as produced, endorsed or verified by Free Law Project.
