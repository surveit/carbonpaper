[[filing_count]] filings cleared the spend filter; [[flagged_count]] ask government for the opposite of what their client committed to in public; [[unmatched_count]] have no public commitment on record.

Invented sample data bundled with this tutorial, and deliberately engineered so the contradictions are easy to see — one short line per filing, one short line per commitment, and names that match exactly. Real filings and real commitments are neither short nor tidy. No row describes a real filing, client, firm or commitment.

Every filing below reported enough lobbying spend to clear this workflow’s filter. Beside it sits what the same client said in public, where a commitment was on record. A filing is marked when it asks government for the opposite of that commitment — and where it is marked, both texts are printed together, so the contradiction is on the page rather than only in the flag. It is a reading aid, not a verdict, and not a finding about the filer.
