What the numbers mean

Review unit
One PR. The critic reads its diff at the commit of the reviewer's first ask whose diff can be rebuilt, and is scored against every ask the reviewer made from then on, on that commit or a later one.
Real comment
An inline comment the labels credit to the reviewer, not the agent posting under their login, whose speech act is a correction, instruction or question, and which is the reviewer's first such comment in its thread.
Exact match
Same file, a real comment left on the commit the critic read, lines at most 5 apart, and the predicted theme one of the real comment's themes.
Judge match
Same file, on any commit, left unmatched by the exact rule, and a judge model shown both comments with the code under each found they make the same point.
Precision / recall / F1
Matched predictions over all predictions; matched real comments over all real comments; their harmonic mean. Matching is one to one. n/a means the denominator is zero.
